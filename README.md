## The Short Version

**What is Roddy Technologies?**
Roddy Technologies Ltd is the parent technology company and corporate brand. [roddytechnologies.com](https://roddytechnologies.com) is the public corporate website and the entry point into the company's ecosystem. The company operates three products: **Roddy Labs**, the operational platform; **Roddy AI**, the intelligence and automation layer; and **RShopRefill**, the consumer-facing digital marketplace.

**What problem is it solving?**
Across many African markets, digital access and digital infrastructure are still harder and more expensive than they should be. Consumers struggle to reliably buy and pay for everyday digital goods. Developers, students, startups, and small businesses struggle to find affordable hosting, domains, tooling, and a competent development team in one place. Most available options are fragmented, imported, priced for other markets, or unsupported locally. We are building the layer that closes that gap.

**What has already been built?**
**[RShopRefill](https://rshoprefill.com) is the company's flagship, and today its only live product.** It is the consumer marketplace, with its catalogue currently focused on digital gift cards, travel eSIMs, mobile airtime, data bundles, and bill-payment services. Alongside it, custom software development and project delivery is an active, revenue-generating line of work, and the corporate website serves as the company's public entry point. Roddy Labs, Roddy AI, and the developer API are in development or planned, and are not yet available.

**What is being built next?**
Roddy Labs as a connected workspace for building and managing digital projects. Roddy AI as an assistance and automation layer across the ecosystem. A developer API that will let approved developers and businesses integrate selected existing Roddy products and services into their own applications.

**Why can this become a meaningful technology company?**
Because each part strengthens the others. RShopRefill provides the consumer distribution layer and the supplier relationships. Roddy Labs provides the operational, infrastructure, development, and B2B layer. Roddy AI provides assistance and automation across both. Shared technology, supplier integrations, infrastructure, and operational knowledge compound over time instead of being rebuilt per project.

## Why We Are Building This

Digital infrastructure in many African markets is available in theory and difficult in practice. The gaps we keep running into are specific, and they are the reason this company exists.

**For consumers.** Buying everyday digital goods is still unreliable. Payment methods fail or are not accepted. International platforms reject local cards or refuse to serve the region entirely. Gift cards, eSIMs, airtime, data, and bill payments are spread across separate providers of uneven quality, with little recourse when a transaction fails.

**For developers.** Hosting, domains, deployment tooling, and API access are typically bought from foreign providers, priced in foreign currency, and paid for with payment methods that are hard to obtain locally. Support is remote and rarely accounts for local conditions. The result is that capable developers spend disproportionate effort on procurement instead of building.

**For students.** Cost is the binding constraint. Affordable infrastructure, practical mentorship, and a real environment to build and host coursework or a first product are scarce. Talent is not the shortage; access is.

**For startups and small businesses.** Getting software built means assembling freelancers across several platforms, coordinating them without technical leadership, and inheriting a codebase nobody is accountable for. Ongoing hosting, domains, maintenance, and support are then someone else's problem again.

**For larger businesses and organisations.** Internal tools, automation, and integration work are often deferred because there is no dependable local partner who can build, deploy, host, and continue to support the result.

Each of these is a distribution problem as much as a technology problem. Solving them separately produces disconnected projects. Solving them on shared infrastructure produces a company.

**Planned.** Not yet public.

The planned Roddy developer API will allow approved developers and businesses to integrate selected existing Roddy digital products and services into their own applications. Scope is deliberately limited at launch to categories the company already supports through RShopRefill.

```bash
$ roddy api --products

bills       ::  utilities, subscriptions, services

auth        ::  bearer token
transport   ::  REST + JSON, webhook callbacks
settlement  ::  wallet balance, mobile money
access      ::  by approval, hello@roddytechnologies.com
```
